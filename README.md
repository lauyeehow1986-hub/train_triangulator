# Singapore Future MRT Station Triangulator & 10-Yr Property ROI WebGIS (`v4.6`)

**Live Interactive WebGIS:** https://lauyeehow1986-hub.github.io/train_triangulator/

An interactive spatial triangulation explorer, 10-year Monte Carlo property investment simulator, **6-Domain 20-Minute Town Livability Index (`v4.5`)**, and **Island-Wide Transit & Livability `Gaps` Auditor (`v4.6`, `0%` MRT weight)** for Singapore, built by fusing public urban planning, transit, procurement, demographic, healthcare, education, HDB resale, official **URA Data Service (`PMI_Resi_Transaction` & `PMI_Resi_Rental_Median`)** caveats, validated **ECDA Infantcare/Childcare + MSF Student Care**, and island-wide **LTA/NParks/NEA/SFA/MOH/HDB** livability layers.

> **Disclaimer:** Triangulated station coordinates, Bayesian posterior scores, 10-year Monte Carlo property gain simulations, and livability/gap indices are statistical spatial estimates derived from public datasets — **not** official Land Transport Authority (LTA) station alignments, government decisions, or financial advice.

## Key Coverage (`v4.6` Island-Wide Gaps Audit + 6-Domain Livability + Family Care + Multi-Anchor + CRL3/TELe + URA REALIS)

- **237 Official MRT/LRT Stations** ([official_mrt_stations.csv](official_mrt_stations.csv) / [official_mrt_stations.geojson](official_mrt_stations.geojson)): `146` Operational MRT, `41` Operational LRT, and `50` Announced Future MRT (`TEL5`, `DTL3e`, `JRL`, `CRL1/CPe`, `CRL2`, `CRL3 CR20–CR23`, `NS3A`, `DTL2e DE1/DE2`, `TE32/CR1 Changi T5`, `TE21 Marina South`, etc.).
- **31 Triangulated Future Unannounced MRT Stations** ([triangulated_future_mrt_stations.csv](triangulated_future_mrt_stations.csv) / [triangulated_future_mrt_stations.geojson](triangulated_future_mrt_stations.geojson)): Covering `Seletar Line`, `Tengah Line`, `JRL West Coast Extension`, independent high-ridership `PV/Bus` rail-gap discoveries, and multi-anchor employment/hospital/education hubs (`>= 700m` heavy-rail gap). `hdb_units_650m` sums exact residential dwelling units via two-pass `(URA planning_area -> HDB town, ST_COD -> street, blk_no)` resolution (`99.72%` 1-to-1 match), excluding `residential = N` MSCPs, food centres, and pavilions.
- **357 Strictly Residential Unannounced-Corridor Properties & 10-Yr MC Simulation** ([property_investment_simulation_postcodes.csv](property_investment_simulation_postcodes.csv) / [benefiting_properties_postal_codes.csv](benefiting_properties_postal_codes.csv)): Deduplicated unique developments (`171` Condo/Apt/EC, `132` HDB including newly reclassified 2024–2026 BTOs, `50` Landed, `4` Residential; zero duplicate postal codes, zero non-market dorms/staff housing/schools/MSCPs). Calibrated via `2,000`-trial 10-year Monte Carlo on `245,441` HDB resale transactions (`2017-01` to `2026-11`), `131,326` official URA private caveats (`192` URA-matched postcodes `<= 800m` with real PSF, `CCR`/`RCR`/`OCR` segments, Freehold vs 99-yr leasehold tenures, and `84` rental matches), SingStat 2017–2026 PPIs, and Data.gov.sg HDB rents (`2026-Q3`).
- **6-Domain 20-Minute Town Livability Index (`0–100`, `0%` MRT Weight) & 5 New Toggleable Overlays:** Orthogonal buyer/own-stay scoring (`Walkability & Covered Linkways 22%`, `Food & Retail 22%`, `Parks, Nature & Fitness 18%`, `Family Care & Primary Health 18%`, `Barrier-Free / Wheelchair Access 10%`, `Smart Mobility, Parking, EV & AV 10%`), with a **Goldilocks expressway rule** (`300–1,500m` ramp positive vs `<120m` carriageway noise penalty) and **environmental disamenity deductions** (expressway carriageway `<120m`, URA `BUSINESS 2` heavy industry `<=300m`, airport/airbase noise `<=1.8km`). Official 2026 **LTA Autonomous Vehicle (AV)** zones cover Punggol public AV shuttles (Routes 1–3), one-north/JID testbeds, and Tengah smart town (`2%` effective cap). **Enrichment centres are excluded (`0%`)** due to lack of an authoritative spatial registry and mall/school collinearity. Includes the **Top 10 Own-Stay (Livability + ROI)** view and multi-criteria sorting/filtering in the WebGIS.
- **Validated ECDA Infantcare/Childcare (`1,869`) & MSF Student Care (`336`) Overlays (`0%` MRT Weight):** Toggleable OFF-by-default WebGIS layers and property-card badges (`Infantcare <= 400m` filter pill in **Postals** tab).
- **Island-Wide Transit Deserts, High-Density Livability Deficits & "Catch-Up" Tab (`Gaps`, `v4.6`, `0%` MRT Weight):** Dedicated 6th WebGIS tab, toggleable `Development & Transit Gaps` overlay, and [development_gaps_rail_and_livability.csv](development_gaps_rail_and_livability.csv) (`90` rows across 4 views): (1) **Top 25 Subzone Heavy-Rail Deserts** scanning all `332` URA subzones by population-weighted distance + unserved bus load (exposing `Residual Deserts` still `>850 m` post-triangulation alongside `Tri-Addressed` subzones), (2) **Top 25 Island-Wide HDB Precinct Rail Gaps** across `10,937` residential HDB blocks (`>=600` units within `300 m`, `>=450 m` separation), (3) **Top 25 High-Density Livability Deficits** across `382` island-wide HDB precinct anchors + major condos (**strictly excluding low-density `Landed` enclaves**) with explicit missing-amenity tags, and (4) **Top 15 "Amenity Catch-Up" Alpha Plays** (low current livability `<56` paired with unbuilt BTO/GLS/Commercial/White/Reserve catalysts `<=800 m` and high MRT posterior `>=72%`).
- **31 Station Area Summaries & 7 2025–2026 BTO Launches** ([station_area_investment_summary.csv](station_area_investment_summary.csv)): Area-level risk-adjusted 10-year gains alongside 5-yr MOP Standard (`0%` clawback) vs 10-yr MOP Prime (`14%` clawback) 4-room BTO exit projections.
- **99 Unbuilt / Future Sites & Reserve Parcels** ([future_unbuilt_sites_and_parcels.csv](future_unbuilt_sites_and_parcels.csv) / [future_unbuilt_sites_and_parcels.geojson](future_unbuilt_sites_and_parcels.geojson)): 2025–2026 HDB BTO / URA GLS sites plus unbuilt URA Master Plan 2025 Reserve Sites, White Sites, and greenfield parcels.

## Integrated Public Data Sources

1. **URA Master Plan 2025 (Oct 2026) & URA Data Service API:** Land-use zoning, Reserve/White Sites, job-weighted Commercial/BP/Industrial GFA (`9,575` parcels), plus official private residential caveats (`PMI_Resi_Transaction` batches `1–4`: `3,820` projects, `131,326` transactions) and `PMI_Resi_Rental_Median` (`937` projects).
2. **SingStat General Household Survey 2025 (via `data.gov.sg`):** Official resident population & age groups across all `332` URA subzones (`4.20M` residents) + Planning Area of Workplace employment (`47` areas).
3. **LTA DataMall:** Oct 2026 `PV/Bus` monthly passenger volumes (`240.3M` tap-ins/tap-outs across `5,203` matched bus stops) + AM peak (`07–09h`) directional origins/destinations, full paginated `RoadWorks` (`8,404` records / `721` geocoded `>=365d` persistent roads), live `TrafficIncidents`, **`GeospatialWholeIsland` (`CoveredLinkWay` `7,183` roofs, `CyclingPath`, `Footpath`, `PedestrainOverheadbridge_UnderPass` lift/ramp flags)**, **`CarParkAvailabilityv2` (`2,615` carparks)**, and **`EVCBatch` (`2,834` EV charging locations)**.
4. **GeBIZ Government Procurement (`data.gov.sg`):** `718` Land Transport Authority tender awards scanned for engineering studies, site investigations, and tunnel/civil interface works.
5. **Healthcare & Education Anchors (OneMap & MOE):** `59` acute/community/private hospitals and public polyclinics (existing + confirmed `2026–2030s` pipeline), `16` Tertiary/IHL campuses (Universities, Polytechnics, ITEs), and `337` MOE schools (`182` Primary / P1–S4 mixed).
6. **ECDA & MSF Family Care Directories (`data.gov.sg`, `0%` MRT Weight):** `1,869` active ECDA centres (`Listing of Centres` + `Listing of Centre Services` fees & `Pre-Schools Location` GeoJSON) and `336` MSF Student Care Centres, coordinate-validated against HDB centroids and OneMap Search API (`850` ECDA GeoJSON + `1,019` OneMap).
7. **20-Minute Town Livability Layers (`data.gov.sg`, Overpass & OneMap, `0%` MRT Weight):** `NParks Parks & Nature Reserves` + `Tracks/PCN`, `NEA Hawker Centres` & `List of Government Markets Hawker Centres` (`129` hubs / `83` wet markets), `SFA/NEA List of Supermarket Licences` (`419/478` resolved), `NEA Licensed Eating Establishments` (`18,685` unique postals/outlets, `959` coffeeshops/foodcourts), `SportSG Facilities` + `HPB Gyms@SG`, `MOH CHAS Clinics` (`1,193`), `NLB Libraries` (`27`), `LTA Silver Zones` (`20`), `MOH Eldercare` (`133`), `HDB Carpark Information` (`2,273`), and OSM validated expressways/ramps/malls/charging stations (`1,376` extra OneMap postal lookups).
8. **HDB & OpenStreetMap:** `13,509` HDB building footprints matched via two-pass `(PA -> HDB town, ST_COD -> street, blk_no)` to official `hdb_property_info` (`10,930` residential, excluding `2,579` non-residential MSCP/hawker/pavilion structures), HDB 2017–Nov 2026 resale transactions, and OSM residential buildings.
